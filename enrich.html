Standalone tool

Email Enrich

Paste or upload a contact list. Get a likely work email per contact, plus a mail check on the domain.

Emails are guesses from common name patterns (first.last, flast, first and so on), ranked best first, with two alternates. The check only confirms the domain can receive mail. It does not confirm the mailbox exists, so verify before a send. Rows run in your browser; names and domains are not stored, and only domains are sent to Cloudflare DNS.
