# DeepEndHQ v5: the operator's product launch

DRAFT proposal for Deep. Deep has NOT approved v5. Preview only.

## 1. Visual Theme and Atmosphere
A personal product studio, not a journal cover or a dashboard. The structural metaphor is a launch sheet: bold proposition, a working product shelf, the twelve-company context, then the published evidence. Stripe supplies sectional rhythm, Framer supplies the large typographic scale and product-forward presentation, Linear supplies restrained surface hierarchy, Vercel supplies precise rules and compact navigation. None of their brand colors are copied.

Audience: founders and marketing operators arriving from Deep's work or social profile. The one belief: Deep works with agents in real marketing operations, not just in presentations. The two objections: generic AI consultancy claims and no visible working output. The single conversion: book the existing free automation call. Strongest supported proof: real public tools, the twelve named companies, 132 actual entries and latest day 354. No results, clients, testimonials or guarantees are invented.

## 2. Color Palette and Roles
Deep's clarified Matrix reset overrides v4 and all Night Ink interpretations. True neutral black ground #080808, surface #111111, card #171717, elevated #202020. Neutral near-white text #F2F6F3, muted #BBC9C0, dim #A1B3A7. Dominant phosphor green #30E060, sparse kintsugi gold #C9A84C for booking fill and thin joining rules only. Light ground #F5F7F6, surface #FFFFFF, card #EDF2EF, raised #E4ECE7. Ink #102219, muted #40594A, dim #4A6052. Text twins green #0F6B33 and gold #725500, never bright green on a light ground. No navy, cream, buttery or olive band. system.css is canonical and scheme-tokens.css remains generated.

## 3. Typography Rules
Self-hosted Inter for oversized product-style display and UI; Fraunces for the distinctly personal editorial reading surfaces and one hero emphasis; JetBrains Mono for actual dates, data and machine logs only. Display 48px phone to 112px wide, negative tracking, line-height 1.02. Section headings 32px to 64px. Body 16px minimum in newly authored surfaces. No new font requests or runtime packages.

## 4. Component Stylings
Build-time source ports of installed 21st Editorial Hero05 and Footer17, existing Arrow Dots booking gesture, and Kokonut WidgetGrid's static item/render presentation grammar. Drag, resize, spring engine, fake sample metrics and dependency-only wrappers are deliberately removed from the marketing shelf. Native A11YPros company disclosures remain accessible and are recomposed as a company-name wall. The real Kokonut terrain remains an island under Machine Logs. Registry failures and exact adaptations are recorded in COMPONENTS.md. No glass folders, testimonial carousel, invented account status or duplicate toolkit.

## 5. Layout Principles
Full-width launch hero with an explicit booking action. One asymmetric tool shelf directly below, featuring real product images rather than a dashboard hero. Company wall, mid-page booking, latest-only log and writing, personal off-hours, machinery, final booking and compact footer. Every section has its own composition. 4px spacing system, 96px maximum section padding, content spans viewport gutters on wide screens. No empty secondary columns. Existing tool data and full-stack disclosure remain the source of truth.

## 6. Depth and Elevation
Near-black grounds and solid luminance-stepped surfaces. Hairline rules, small radii, no content blur. Images show actual product pages, not fabricated product results. Gold belongs to conversion, not to arbitrary stamps. MicroKit-inspired arrow movement and outline response provide one interaction per control.

## 7. Do's and Don'ts
Keep scouter bytes, immutable content, backend, migrations, greeting-only .dh-pro suppression and system-theme controller. Keep 44px targets, focus-visible rings, static content with JS off. Do not fabricate client proof, guarantees, outcomes, account activity or freshness. Booking copy is visibly labelled DRAFT in this preview. No production merge/deploy. No testimonial-free proof made to look like a testimonial.

## 8. Responsive Behavior
Device class: pointer and touch web. Binding width 375px; also 768, 1280, 1600, both schemes. Mobile order: proposition, identity, booking, tools. Grids shrink with minmax(0,1fr), then stack. Typography compresses without truncation. Company disclosures and tools work without JS. Motion budget: 400ms entrance using transform only, 200ms interactive arrow, existing finite terrain. Reduced motion is a complete static layout; no expressive WebGL tier. Check real element rectangles, not a root overflow clip.

## 9. Agent Prompt Guide
Make Deep's actual build habit visible before asking for the call. Use strong type, real products, honest dated writing and green on near-black, with gold reserved for booking. The scouter is the identity, the tools are the proof, the free call is the destination. Keep the static prerender plus React islands architecture. This is a proposal for explicit visual approval, not a publication request.
